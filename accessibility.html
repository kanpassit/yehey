Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 5, 2026

KanPassIt is committed to making our exam prep app usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we have done

  • Full keyboard operation of quizzes, menus and dialogs, with visible focus.
  • Screen reader labels, headings, landmarks and announcements for quiz feedback.
  • Text contrast and sizing that meet AA requirements, and support for zooming.
  • Answer states shown with text as well as color.
  • Support for reduced-motion settings.

Known limitations

  • Third-party advertising content (Google AdSense) is outside our control and may not be fully accessible.
  • This is an ongoing effort, and some content may not yet meet every guideline.

Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of KanPassIt, or need content in another format, please tell us and we will work to fix it promptly.
